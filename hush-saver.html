About

Secret Saver shows what a members-only fuel discount app looks like: a pass to show at the counter, a map of stations and a list of offers.

It is a demo. The barcode is a sample that will not scan, the offers are examples and nothing here is connected to a fuel retailer.

The Fuel Finder opens PetrolSpy for live prices.

How to redeem

  1. Show your pass at the counter before you pay.
  2. The attendant scans the barcode.
  3. The discount comes off your fuel.

Demo steps only. This pass will not scan.
